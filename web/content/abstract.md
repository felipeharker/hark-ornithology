This project catalogs and shares birding observations from around the world, with
checklists and media hosted on eBird and the Macaulay Library. It began as an experiment
merging a love of birds with web development, and remains under active, frequent
development.

Every figure on this page is derived from exported eBird data rather than entered by hand:
the map plots recorded coordinates, the location index counts observations per site, and
each checklist report reproduces a single submitted list.
