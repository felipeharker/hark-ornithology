Here is a catalog of the resources, field guides, and documents by location and topic:

**General North America & Pelagic/At-Sea**

* American Ornithological Society (AOS) Check-list of North American Birds: [https://checklist.americanornithology.org/taxa/](https://checklist.americanornithology.org/taxa/).


* National Audubon Society Field Guide to Birds of North America: [https://www.audubon.org/national-audubon-society-field-guides](https://www.audubon.org/national-audubon-society-field-guides).


* Avibase (Database for generating regional and pelagic checklists): [https://avibase.bsc-eoc.org/](https://avibase.bsc-eoc.org/).



**Washington State**

* WOS Official Checklist PDF: [https://wos.org/documents/WBRC/2021/WA-checklist-Nov2021.pdf](https://wos.org/documents/WBRC/2021/WA-checklist-Nov2021.pdf).


* Washington Birder County Checklists: [http://www.wabirder.com/county_map.html](http://www.wabirder.com/county_map.html).



**Victoria, British Columbia**

* Victoria Natural History Society (VNHS) Checklist of Birds for Victoria & Southeastern Vancouver Island: [http://www.vicnhs.bc.ca/attachments/checklist_2017.pdf](http://www.vicnhs.bc.ca/attachments/checklist_2017.pdf).


* Nature Guide to the Victoria Region (Edited by Ann Nightingale and Claudia Copley): [https://www.vicnhs.bc.ca/?page_id=1441](https://www.vicnhs.bc.ca/?page_id=1441).



**Southeast Alaska (Macro-Regional)**

* Southeast Alaska Birding Trail (Tongass National Forest hotspot guide): [https://www.juneauaudubon.org/birding/](https://www.juneauaudubon.org/birding/).


* Checklist of Southeast Alaska Birds (Steve C. Heinl, 2010): [https://www.juneauaudubon.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/SoutheastAlaska_Heinl_2010.pdf](https://www.juneauaudubon.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/SoutheastAlaska_Heinl_2010.pdf).


* Checklist of Alaska Birds (University of Alaska Museum): [https://www.universityofalaskamuseumbirds.org/products/checklist.pdf](https://www.universityofalaskamuseumbirds.org/products/checklist.pdf).



**Ketchikan & Ward Cove, Alaska**

* Ketchikan Wildlife Viewing - Ward Lake Area (Alaska Department of Fish and Game): [https://www.adfg.alaska.gov/index.cfm?adfg=southeastviewing.ketchikanwardlakearea](https://www.adfg.alaska.gov/index.cfm?adfg=southeastviewing.ketchikanwardlakearea).


* Ketchikan Bird Viewing PDF (Alaska Department of Fish and Game): [https://www.adfg.alaska.gov/static/viewing/pdfs/ketchikan.pdf](https://www.adfg.alaska.gov/static/viewing/pdfs/ketchikan.pdf).



**Juneau, Alaska**

* Juneau Audubon Society Birding Resources (Includes links to "Notes for Visiting Birders" and "A Starter Guide to Birding in Juneau"): [https://www.juneauaudubon.org/birding/](https://www.juneauaudubon.org/birding/).


* Birds of Juneau, Alaska Checklist (Juneau Audubon Society): [https://www.juneauaudubon.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/JUNEAU-Checklist-Rev-Jan-2007.pdf](https://www.juneauaudubon.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/JUNEAU-Checklist-Rev-Jan-2007.pdf).



**Skagway, Alaska (Klondike Gold Rush National Historical Park)**

* Birds in and around Skagway, Alaska (National Park Service): [https://www.nps.gov/klgo/learn/nature/birds.htm](https://www.nps.gov/klgo/learn/nature/birds.htm).


* Climate Change Vulnerability Assessment for Birds at Klondike Gold Rush National Historical Park (NPS): [https://www.nps.gov/subjects/climatechange/upload/KLGO_2018_Birds_-_CC_508Compliant.pdf](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/climatechange/upload/KLGO_2018_Birds_-_CC_508Compliant.pdf).
