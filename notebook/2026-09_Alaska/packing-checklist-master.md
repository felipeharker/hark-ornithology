# Master Checklist: Birding & Photography Expedition

This checklist is optimized for travel by air and sea, followed by on-foot field tracking. It prioritizes equipment protection from maritime elements and mobility for long treks.

## 1. Photography Gear (Core & Electronics)

### Cameras & Lenses
- [ ] **Primary Camera Body:** (Fast continuous shooting, robust weather-sealing)
- [ ] **Backup Camera Body:** (Essential for remote locations where repairs are impossible)
- [ ] **Super-Telephoto Lens:** (e.g., 400mm, 500mm, 600mm, or a 150-600mm zoom for birding)
- [ ] **Medium Telephoto Lens:** (e.g., 70-200mm for environmental portraits and larger flocks)
- [ ] **Wide-Angle Lens:** (e.g., 16-35mm or 24-70mm for habitat and landscape documentation)
- [ ] **Teleconverters:** (1.4x and/or 2.0x for extended reach)

### Power & Storage
- [ ] **Primary Memory Cards:** (High-capacity, fast write speeds like CFexpress/SD UHS-II)
- [ ] **Backup Memory Cards:** (Sufficient capacity to avoid formatting in the field)
- [ ] **Rugged Memory Card Case:** (Water-resistant and crushproof)
- [ ] **External SSDs:** (At least two rugged, bus-powered drives for redundant daily backups)
- [ ] **High-Speed Card Reader:** (With appropriate cables for your laptop/tablet)
- [ ] **OEM Camera Batteries:** (Minimum 3-4 per body; cold sea air drains batteries faster)
- [ ] **Dual-Slot Battery Chargers:** (To maximize charging efficiency overnight)
- [ ] **High-Capacity Power Bank:** (For charging devices on long transit legs)
- [ ] **Universal Power Adapters/Surge Protector:** (For sea vessel or international outlets)

### Filters & Accessories
- [ ] **Clear/UV Protection Filters:** (Crucial for protecting front elements from corrosive sea spray)
- [ ] **Circular Polarizer (CPL):** (To cut through water glare when photographing pelagic birds)
- [ ] **Robust Tripod:** (Carbon fiber to minimize weight during hikes, resistant to salt water corrosion)
- [ ] **Gimbal Tripod Head:** (For smooth tracking of birds in flight with heavy lenses)
- [ ] **Quick Release Plates:** (Extra plates in case one goes missing)
- [ ] **Rain Covers:** (Custom-fit waterproof sleeves for your specific telephoto setup)
- [ ] **Remote Shutter Release:** (Wired or wireless)
- [ ] **Cleaning Kit:** (Air blower, sensor swabs, lens fluid, large supply of microfiber cloths)

---

## 2. Travel & Transport Equipment

### Luggage & Cases
- [ ] **Hardcase for Core Electronics:** (ATA-rated, watertight, pressure-equalized case like a Pelican for carry-on air travel and safe sea transport)
- [ ] **Expedition Camera Backpack:** (Ergonomic, weather-resistant bag for the on-foot segments of the trip; must fit comfortably fully loaded)
- [ ] **Packable Duffel/Dry Bag:** (For organizing non-electronic gear or wrapping the backpack during wet Zodiac sea landings)

### Accessory Organization
- [ ] **Cable Organizer Pouches:** (Dedicated clamshell or roll-up cases for charging cables and adapters)
- [ ] **Desiccant Packets:** (To throw into cases and bags to combat extreme humidity/sea moisture)
- [ ] **Neoprene Lens Pouches:** (For extra shock absorption inside the backpack)
- [ ] **Small Waterproof Dry Bags:** (For quickly stashing batteries or your phone during sudden squalls)

---

## 3. Field Gear & Personal Observation Items

### Observation & Documentation
- [ ] **High-End Binoculars:** (8x42 or 10x42 configuration, nitrogen-purged and waterproof)
- [ ] **Binocular Harness:** (To distribute weight off the neck during long hikes)
- [ ] **Waterproof Field Notebook:** (e.g., Rite in the Rain for logging sightings in damp conditions)
- [ ] **All-Weather Pens/Pencils:** (Pressurized ink cartridges that write through moisture)
- [ ] **Regional Field Guides:** (Physical, weatherproofed book specific to your destination)
- [ ] **Digital Field Guides/Audio Apps:** (Downloaded for offline use)

### Navigation & Utilities
- [ ] **Satellite Messenger / PLB:** (e.g., Garmin inReach for emergencies in off-grid maritime or wilderness zones)
- [ ] **Multi-Tool:** (With hex keys and screwdrivers for tightening tripod legs or camera plates)
- [ ] **Headlamp with Red-Light Mode:** (For hands-free operation and preserving night vision during pre-dawn starts)
- [ ] **Compass & Topographic Map:** (Analog backups to digital navigation)
